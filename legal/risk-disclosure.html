General Risk

Forex and CFD trading involves significant risk and may not be suitable for every investor. You may lose some or all of the capital committed to trading.

Leverage & Margin

Leverage can magnify gains and losses. Margin requirements may change and positions can be closed when available margin is insufficient.

Market Volatility

Prices can move rapidly because of economic releases, geopolitical events, changes in liquidity and other market factors.

Execution & Liquidity

Execution conditions can vary with liquidity, volatility, technology and the specific product or execution model described in the approved client documentation.

Costs & Financing

Trading may involve spreads, commissions, financing, conversion costs and other charges. Review the applicable trading conditions before trading.

Technology & Connectivity

Internet, device, software and communication failures can delay or prevent order transmission or access to trading services.

Independent Advice

Website information should not be treated as personal investment advice. Investors should understand the products and risks and seek independent advice where appropriate.
