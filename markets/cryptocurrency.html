Crypto Knowledge

Understand what can move crypto markets.

A disciplined trading process starts with understanding the digital-asset market, its volatility and the factors that can influence prices.

What moves crypto prices?

Market sentiment, liquidity, macroeconomic conditions, industry news and changes in demand can all influence cryptocurrency prices.

Why risk management matters

Cryptocurrency markets can be highly volatile. Position sizing, stop-loss planning and careful leverage management are important parts of a responsible approach.

Understanding market cycles

Digital assets can experience strong shifts in momentum and sentiment. Traders should consider both broader market context and instrument-specific behaviour.
